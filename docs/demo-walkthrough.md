# A partial correction, then a scoped decision

Use fictional material only. The shared demo has no authentication. Seeded cases contain authored reviewer findings; uploading the same files as a fresh submission does not generate findings automatically. Case references can vary, so open **Make room for what’s next** by title.

1. **Inspect the starting package.** In the personal-loan case, compare `social-ad.png` with the supporting offer reference. The original fee claim conflicts with that fictional reference, and the destination rendition is missing. Confirm which files are creative versus internal evidence.
2. **Prepare actionable feedback.** Open **Prepare feedback** and inspect the requested actions. Saving produces a **Prepared** draft; nothing is sent. **Mark waiting** records who owes the next action. Internal notes stay out of generated replies.
3. **Make a partial correction.** Download the revised image from **Sample materials**, or use [loan/v2/social-ad.png](../public/fixtures/loan/v2/social-ad.png). Choose **Add revision**. Uncheck the old image, retain the offer reference, and upload the new image as **Creative**. Describe the correction, save, and **Confirm package**. The filename can remain `social-ad.png`; the earlier bytes remain in version history.
4. **Reconcile, rather than clear everything.** Inspect the new image and resolve only the fee finding with a reason. The destination finding remains open. **Record decision** should show that approval is blocked. The **Versions** view lets you compare the actual packages.
5. **Complete the package.** Add another revision, retaining the corrected image and offer reference. Upload [loan/v3/destination.pdf](../public/fixtures/loan/v3/destination.pdf) with the **Destination** role. The social image is unchanged, so it need not be uploaded again. Confirm the package and inspect both PDF pages before resolving the remaining finding.
6. **Decide and retrieve.** Record the reviewed package's intended use, rationale, and human-review acknowledgment. Approval applies only to that version. Prepare the decision reply separately, reload, and inspect the preserved decision and versions.

Afterward, open **Activity & notes → Download record**. The browser-tested action downloads a ZIP containing original files, version/context records, findings, decisions, timestamps, and captured decision snapshots where available. It excludes note records and reply drafts but includes internal reviewer reasoning. Treat it as an internal review record, not an external response or production audit system.

For a blank review, choose **New submission** and upload your own fictional PNG/JPEG/PDF package. Credit cards and mortgage prequalification use the same human workflow. Uploads permit 10 new files, 10 MB per file, and 25 MB total. Other formats are kept for manual download; they are not automatically previewed or assessed. The [fixture guide](../public/fixtures/README.md) explains the authored evidence and other scenarios.
