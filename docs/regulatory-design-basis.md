# What the compliance research changed

The product is grounded in specific advertising requirements and documented review procedures. They informed **what a reviewer needs to inspect and preserve**: the actual creative, the applicable offer, evidence for a claim, and the version receiving a decision. They are not encoded as automatic compliance checks. The software manages review; a saved approval is a person's decision, not a legal certification.

Sources below were checked on September 27, 2026. ClearPath's legal entity, exact products, and internal policies are fictional and unspecified. Applicability remains a reviewer responsibility. The [research narrative](research.md) explains the broader investigation and bottleneck hypothesis; this document maps evidence to implemented choices.

## Evidence from real review procedures

[Affirm's custom-marketing submission instructions](https://businesshub.affirm.com/hc/en-us/articles/6402545332628-Submitting-Custom-Marketing-Assets-to-Affirm), updated February 24, 2026, ask for campaign context, dates, audience, medium, and creative or landing-page material. They describe email feedback followed by revised assets in the reply. This supports treating a submission as a **package of material and context**, and supporting a complete correction round. It does not establish the most common format or ClearPath's actual working practices.

[GreenSky's Marketing Compliance Guide](https://cms.greensky.com/docs/core/guidelines/marketing-compliance-guide.pdf#page=4), April 8, 2026, page 4, asks its participating merchants to submit materials before publication and resubmit requested changes for final approval. That informed preserved versions, explicit finding dispositions, and decisions tied to the reviewed package. These are GreenSky's procedures for its program, not a universal statutory requirement for per-ad signoff.

Our submission portal, separate return page, and deliberate sharing of feedback implement that round trip. Choosing a portal before email integration was a take-home scope decision made with the user. Research identifies the additional transfer effort that choice leaves; it does not prove affiliates prefer portals.

## Retention is not one rule

[Regulation Z §1026.25(a)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/25/) expressly excludes advertising under §§1026.16 and 1026.24 from its general two-year evidence-retention rule. Conversely, [Regulation N §1014.5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1014#1014.5) requires covered persons to retain specified mortgage communications and product records for 24 months after the last relevant dissemination. That is a scoped obligation with a different trigger, not a rule for every ClearPath case.

The app preserves versions and decision records and provides a portable internal export. It does not track last dissemination, implement a complete retention schedule or legal holds, or establish tamper resistance. Keeping a ZIP is useful retrieval, not demonstrated retention compliance.

## Where product judgment starts

The queue, next owner, recoverable drafts, separate audiences, and unresolved-finding gate are our coordination controls. No source above mandates this screen layout, these states, human approval of every ad, or a prohibition on AI. We chose them because losing a correction, inspecting the wrong evidence, or carrying approval onto changed material is costly within the selected workflow.

The [project rationale](product-story.md) follows the interview's emphasis on completing consequential interactions and explaining deliberate omissions. We deferred automatic substantive review because the assignment establishes an Excel/email bottleneck without measuring analysis effort, and this project has no representative evaluation set showing reliable detection or net time saved. That is a scope and evidence decision, not a claim that automation cannot work.

Supporting three product labels does not implement every rule for those products. There is no maintained legal-rule library, comprehensive state-law coverage, automatic disclosure verification, or post-publication monitoring. The defensible claim is narrower: research shaped a working place for people to inspect evidence, request corrections, and preserve a decision about specific material.
